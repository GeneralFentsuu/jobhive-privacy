Delete My JobHive Account

You have the right to delete your JobHive account and all associated personal data at any time.

Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data — including your profile, job history, and messages — will be permanently removed.

How to request deletion

Send an email to us with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address registered to your JobHive account.

bearowltech@outlook.com

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and login credentials
  • Your job postings and job history
  • Your in-app messages
  • Your phone number and personal details
  • Any ID documents or selfies (if applicable)

What may be retained

We may retain certain transaction records where required by law. These records will not contain your personal identifying information.

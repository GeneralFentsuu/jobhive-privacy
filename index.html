3. How we use your data

We use your data to operate the JobHive platform — to create your account, verify provider identity, match jobs to available providers, and enable communication between matched parties.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law.

4. ID documents and selfies

ID images and selfies submitted for provider verification are stored in a private, encrypted storage bucket. They are accessible only to BEAROWL administrators for the purpose of manual verification. ID images are permanently deleted within 24 hours of account approval. Only the outcome of verification is retained — approval status, date, and approver.

5. Data security

All data is stored in Supabase, protected by Row Level Security. No user can access another user's data. The poster's phone number is never visible to providers under any circumstance.

6. Data retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at bearowltech@outlook.com. ID images are deleted within 24 hours of verification regardless of any other retention.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at bearowltech@outlook.com to exercise these rights.

8. Children

JobHive is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. We will notify users of significant changes through the app.
